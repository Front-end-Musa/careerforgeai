## 💡 Pro Tips for Ongoing SEO

### Content Creation

- Write 1 blog post per month on job/resume topics
- Aim for 1,500-2,000 word count
- Include H2s, H3s, and lists for featured snippets
- Internal link to main pages

### Keyword Research

- Check Google Search Console monthly
- Look for keywords with high impressions but low CTR
- Improve titles/descriptions for those pages
- Add similar keywords to new content

### User Engagement

- Collect more testimonials/reviews
- Display rating prominently
- Ask happy users to leave Google reviews
- Update rating in schema regularly

### Technical Health

- Monitor Core Web Vitals (Page Speed Insights)
- Check for broken links monthly
- Ensure mobile responsiveness
- Test new content with Lighthouse

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: When will rankings improve?

**A:** Give it 2-6 weeks. Schema improvements show faster (2-4 weeks). Rankings take longer as Google crawls and indexes your site.

### Q: Should I rewrite old content?

**A:** Yes. Update content that's 6+ months old. Refresh statistics, add new insights, improve keyword targeting.

### Q: Can I add more keywords?

**A:** Yes, but focus on quality. Add 2-3 keywords per page max. Natural language > keyword stuffing.

### Q: What about competitor research?

**A:** Monitor top 3 ranking competitors monthly. Use SEMrush/Ahrefs to identify gaps in their strategy.

### Q: How do I get more reviews?

**A:** Add Google review link in email, add reviews request after conversion, incentivize feedback.
